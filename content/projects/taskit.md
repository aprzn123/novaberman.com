Taskit is a TUI program that I built mostly for myself. It tracks how much time I spend on everything, from class assignments to personal projects to job applications. Development is ongoing in the background, but even in its current state I know a couple of people who have been using it and have found it beneficial for their own workflows.

I plan on coming back to this page later to update it and talk more about its development, but for now I welcome you to take a peek at the source code and the write-up in the README :)
